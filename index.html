Designed with restraint

AI should support what you mean—not decide it for you.

ezTalk is built to reduce correction effort while keeping the person communicating in control. AI suggestions and transformations are assistive outputs, not a substitute for the user’s judgment.

  • The user remains responsible for reviewing and choosing the final message.
  • Correction is intended to clarify language, not invent unexpressed intent.
  • Text-to-speech is optional and used only when the user chooses it.
